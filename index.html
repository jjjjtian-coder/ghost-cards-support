抽鬼牌

两个玩家，同一台设备。找到鬼牌的位置，换牌，再交给对方抽。

怎么玩

开局先展示鬼牌，再插入展开的牌组，总共 7 张。换位玩家能看见牌面，拖动牌调整位置，完成后点“好了，交给对方”。抽牌玩家看到牌背，可点选后确认，也可将牌向上拖出足够距离。抽到普通牌会移走一张并交换角色，重新展示鬼牌位置；抽到鬼牌就输。只剩最后一张鬼牌时，下一位抽牌玩家直接输。

只有一面屏幕，也能玩

按照提示把设备交给对应玩家，拿到设备后再继续。双屏功能在支持的设备上可用，并单独征求相机权限。拒绝或撤销相机权限仍可在一块屏幕上轮流玩。

没有声音

确认设备没有静音，并调整媒体音量。游戏切到后台或系统音频被打断时会暂停声音。

App support

Ghost Cards

Two players, one device. Watch the ghost card, rearrange the cards, then let the other player pick.

How to play

Each game starts by showing the ghost before inserting it among the six other cards, making a seven-card spread. The player arranging the cards can see their faces and drag them into new positions, then tap “Ready, your turn.” The picker sees card backs and can select a card and confirm, or drag it upward far enough to play it. A safe card is removed and the players swap roles, with the ghost position shown again. Picking the ghost loses. If only the ghost remains, the next picker loses immediately.

Playing on one screen

Pass the device to the player shown by the handoff prompt, then continue after receiving it. On supported devices, optional dual-display play requests camera permission separately. Declining or revoking camera access still allows pass-and-play on one screen.

No sound

Check silent mode and the media volume. Audio pauses when the game enters the background or system audio is interrupted.
