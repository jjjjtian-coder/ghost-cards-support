隐私政策

抽鬼牌 · Ghost Cards

发布者：Thunderstone Co., Ltd.
更新日期：2026-11-10

数据与账户

抽鬼牌不要求注册账户，不向开发者或第三方发送个人数据。游戏不包含广告、内购、行为统计或跨应用跟踪。

本机数据

牌序、角色和当前对局在本机处理，不上传或共享。应用仅在本机保存你对双屏功能的选择。删除应用会删除该应用的本机设置。我们没有服务器上的用户档案需要删除。

可选的相机权限

在支持的设备上，双屏功能需要系统相机会话来呈现另一面的牌桌。启用前会说明并请求你的许可。相机画面不用于游戏判断，不显示、不保存、不录制、不分析，也不上传。应用不访问麦克风。单屏轮流玩法不需要相机权限。

你的选择

你可以拒绝相机权限，继续使用单屏轮流玩法；也可以随时在 iPhone 的“设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 相机”中撤销权限。撤销后重新进入应用，将使用不需要相机的玩法。

网络与外部链接

游戏及其图片、声音和动画均随应用提供，游戏过程不需要联网。你主动打开隐私、支持或许可链接时，浏览器会连接相应网站，适用该网站的隐私政策。

主动联系支持

若你主动发送支持邮件，你的邮箱、邮件内容和主动附上的文件会提供给我们，用于回应你的问题。邮件由邮件服务商处理，适用其服务与隐私条款。游戏不会自动附送对局内容或相机画面。

儿童与政策更新

游戏不会自动收集玩家（包括儿童）的个人数据。若未来版本改变数据处理方式，会更新本说明，并在需要时重新征求你的许可。

本网站与托管服务

本网站由 GitHub Pages 提供静态页面托管。访问网站时，浏览器会向 GitHub 发送加载网页所需的 IP 地址和请求信息，并可能包含浏览器及设备信息。

GitHub 明确说明，会为安全目的记录并保存 GitHub Pages 访客的 IP 地址，无论访客是否登录 GitHub。相关技术访问数据的处理和保存遵循 GitHub 的隐私政策。

本网站不设置 Cookie，不使用浏览器本地存储，也不包含访问统计、广告或跟踪脚本。我们没有在网站上设置账户、表单或自动上传游戏数据的功能。

Privacy and contact

Privacy policy

Ghost Cards

Publisher: Thunderstone Co., Ltd.
Updated: 2026-11-10

Data and accounts

Ghost Cards does not require an account or send personal data to the developer or third parties. The game has no advertising, in-app purchases, analytics, or cross-app tracking.

Data on your device

Card order, player roles, and the current round are processed on your device and are not uploaded or shared. The app stores your dual-display preference locally. Deleting the app removes its local settings. We hold no server-side user profile to delete.

Optional camera permission

On supported devices, the dual-display feature uses a system camera session to present the other card table. Its purpose is explained and your permission is requested before use. Camera frames are not used for gameplay, displayed, saved, recorded, analyzed, or uploaded. The app does not access the microphone. Pass-and-play on one screen does not need camera permission.

Your choices

You can decline camera access and continue playing on one screen. You can revoke access at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera. When you reopen the app after revoking access, play continues without the camera.

Network and external links

The game and its images, sounds, and animations are included in the app. Gameplay does not need an internet connection. If you choose to open a privacy, support, or license link, your browser connects to that website and its privacy policy applies.

Contacting support

If you choose to email support, you provide us with your email address, message, and any attachments you include, so that we can respond. Email is processed by the email service provider under its terms and privacy policy. The game does not automatically attach gameplay data or camera images.

Children and policy updates

The game does not automatically collect personal data from players, including children. If a future version changes how data is handled, this notice will be updated and your permission will be requested when required.

This website and its hosting

This website is a static site hosted by GitHub Pages. When you visit, your browser sends GitHub the IP address and request information needed to load the pages, which may include browser and device information.

GitHub states that it logs and stores the IP addresses of GitHub Pages visitors for security purposes, whether or not they are signed in to GitHub. GitHub handles and retains this technical access data under its privacy policy.

This website does not set cookies, use browser local storage, or include analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts. We do not provide accounts, forms, or automatic uploads of game data on this website.
